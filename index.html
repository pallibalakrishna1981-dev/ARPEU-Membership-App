WHO WE ARE

Andhra Rastra Power Employees Union (ARPEU) was established on 06th July 1982. Regd. No. G-445, affiliated to Bharatiya Mazdoor Sangh (BMS).

ARPEU is the collective voice of power sector employees in Andhra Pradesh, committed to protecting their rights and promoting their welfare.
